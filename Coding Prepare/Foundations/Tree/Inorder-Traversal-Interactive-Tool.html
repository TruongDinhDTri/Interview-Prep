💡 Why Inorder Matters

  • In a Binary Search Tree (BST), inorder traversal gives nodes in ascending order
  • It's often used to "flatten" a tree into a sorted sequence
  • Inorder is natural for expression trees (think of mathematical expressions)
  • It's the basis for many tree algorithms and transformations

✨ Key Characteristic

The defining feature of inorder traversal is that we visit a node between its left and right subtrees. This "in-between" timing is what gives inorder traversal its name.

✨ Recursive Elegance

The recursive implementation is remarkably elegant and mirrors the definition of inorder traversal perfectly:

  1. First traverse the left subtree
  2. Then visit the root node
  3. Finally traverse the right subtree

The call stack naturally keeps track of where we are in the traversal, allowing us to "pause" at each node, process its left subtree, visit the node itself, and then process its right subtree.

💡 Mastering the Mental Model

🤔 The Core Question: "Why do we need a Pointer AND a Stack?"

The Stack remembers the History (where we came from).
The Pointer (current) represents our Intention (where we want to go).

🏃 Phase 1: The Explorer

Trigger: current has a Node

You are standing at a node. In Inorder, you can't take a photo (visit) yet! You must go Left first.

  • 1. 📝 Bookmarking: "I'll save this spot (Push to Stack) to finish later."
  • 2. 📉 Diving: "Now I move Left." (current = current.left)
Goal: Reach the bottom-left-most node possible.

📸 Phase 2: The Photographer

Trigger: current is Null (Hit a Wall)

You tried to go left but hit a dead end (None). This means the parent (last bookmark) is ready!

  • 1. 🔙 Backtracking: "Return to last bookmark." (Pop from Stack)
  • 2. 📸 Visiting: "Take the photo now!" (Add to Result)
  • 3. ➡️ Switching: "Now check the Right side." (current = node.right)

✨ The "Aha!" Moment

The algorithm is just an infinite loop of Exploring (pushing/going left) until you hit a wall, then switching to Photographing (popping/visiting) and moving one step Right to start Exploring again.
