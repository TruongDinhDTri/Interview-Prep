💡 What is a Queue?
A Queue is like a line at Totoro's bus stop - first one there, first one on the bus! Elements enter from the back (enqueue) and leave from the front (dequeue).

Deque (Double-Ended Queue) is extra magical - you can add or remove from both ends with O(1) time complexity, making it perfect for sliding window problems!
