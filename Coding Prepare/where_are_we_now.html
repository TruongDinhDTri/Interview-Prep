Path Forward #2
The Translation Block

You spent years training your hands to code. Now train your mouth to speak. This 2-week bootcamp builds the bridge between your brain and your voice.

    What questions made you freeze? Write better answers in ANSWER_CARDS.md. Expand your catalog.
  • 14
    Day 14: Full mock interview (30 min)
    Complete mock: behavioral + technical + project discussion. Record it. Listen back. You'll be shocked how much better you are.
