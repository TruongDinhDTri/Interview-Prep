🧠 Core Ideas from Your Teacher

🎯 The Real Problem

You have a map. Some spots are wells (0). Some spots are houses (1).

👉 For each house, how far is the nearest well?

❌ The Wrong Instinct

You're thinking: "To find the nearest well for a house... shouldn't I start from the house and search outward?"

That's what any normal human would think. That's why you're confused.

✅ The Trick - Flip Your Thinking!

🏠 Houses should NOT search for wells.
💧 Wells should search for houses.

❌ Don't: Start from each house looking for the closest well

✅ Do: Start from all wells spreading outward to the houses

☕ The Coffee Shop Analogy

Your neighborhood has 10 wells but 300 houses.

❌ The Slow Way:

300 different houses all walking around searching for wells → takes forever

✅ The Fast Way:

10 wells send messengers outward at the same time. First messenger to reach a house tells it the distance.

🌈 The Key Realization

It is easier and faster to let FEW sources reach MANY targets
than to let MANY targets search for FEW sources.

You flipped the direction. That's the entire issue.

🧨 The Simplest Statement

Instead of asking every house:

"Where's the closest well?"

You ask the wells:

"Go touch every house and tell them how long it took."

That's BFS from all 0s. Nothing more. 🎯
