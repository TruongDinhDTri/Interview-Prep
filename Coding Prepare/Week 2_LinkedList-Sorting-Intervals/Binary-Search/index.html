Divide and Conquer - The Art of Efficient Search

💡 About Binary Search

Binary Search is one of the most fundamental and powerful algorithms in computer science. By repeatedly dividing the search space in half, we achieve O(log n) time complexity - making it incredibly efficient even for massive datasets.

Key Insight: Every time we compare and move our pointers, we're eliminating half of the remaining possibilities. This is the power of divide and conquer! 🔥
