# Mock Behavioral Interview Guide

Use this guide to run mock behavioral interviews with Hadriel or a practice partner.

---

## Mock Interview Structure (30 minutes)

| Phase | Duration | Content |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **Intro** | 2 min | Self-introduction (elevator pitch) |
| **Questions** | 20 min | 5-6 behavioral questions, ~3 min each |
| **Your Questions** | 5 min | Ask interviewer 2-3 questions |
| **Debrief** | 3 min | Feedback and scoring |

---

## Question Selection

For each mock, pick questions that test different dimensions:

### Balanced Mock Set (covers all 8 dimensions)
1. "Tell me about yourself" → *Communication, Motivation*
2. "Tell me about your most impactful project" → *Proactivity, Company Values*
3. "Describe a time you dealt with an ambiguous situation" → *Unstructured Env*
4. "Tell me about a conflict with a colleague" → *Conflict & Empathy*
5. "Tell me about a time you failed" → *Growth, Perseverance*
6. "Why do you want to work here?" → *Motivation, Company Values*

### Technical-Heavy Mock Set
1. "What's the most challenging technical problem you solved?"
2. "Tell me about a project that didn't go as planned"
3. "How do you stay current with technology?"
4. "Describe a time you had to learn something quickly"
5. "What's the most interesting project you've worked on?"

### Leadership-Heavy Mock Set
1. "Give me an example of when you showed leadership"
2. "How do you mentor or help junior developers?"
3. "Tell me about a time you pushed for something"
4. "How do you handle disagreements with your manager?"
5. "What would you do in your first 90 days?"

---

## Scoring Rubric (per answer)

Score each answer on a 1-5 scale:

| Criteria | 1 (Weak) | 3 (Adequate) | 5 (Strong) |
|----------|----------|-------------|------------|
| **Structure** | No clear STAR(R) | Has STAR but unclear flow | Clean STAR(R), natural flow |
| **Specificity** | Vague, generic | Some details | Rich details, names, numbers |
| **Action Focus** | Mostly "we" | Mix of "I" and "we" | Strong "I" focus throughout |
| **Impact** | No metrics | Some qualitative impact | Quantified results, clear ROI |
| **Reflection** | No reflection | Brief takeaway | Deep principle + future application |
| **Seniority Signal** | Individual scope | Team scope | Team/org scope + mentorship |
| **Delivery** | Nervous, filler words | Steady, some hesitation | Confident, natural, engaging |

### Overall Score
- **30-35/35:** Interview-ready
- **25-29/35:** Strong, needs minor polish
- **20-24/35:** Good foundation, practice more
- **Below 20:** Needs significant refinement

## Common Mistakes to Watch For

1. **The Rambler** — S+T takes over 1 minute (should be 30 sec)
2. **The "We" Trap** — Using "we" in the Action section instead of "I"
3. **The Skipped Reflection** — Forgetting to add the R(eflection) at the end
4. **The Memorizer** — Sounds rehearsed and robotic, not natural
5. **The Avoider** — Gives a different story when pressed for details
6. **The Humble Brag** — "My biggest weakness is I work too hard"
7. **No Numbers** — Results without quantification ("it was better")
