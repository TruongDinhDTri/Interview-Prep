# Questions to Ask Interviewers

**Source:** [Tech Interview Handbook — Final Questions](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/final-questions/)

**Always prepare 3-5 questions.** Pick from different categories to show breadth of thinking.

---

## Wiganz's Go-To Combo (Pick 3)

For most interviews, use this combination:

1. **"What's the most important problem I'd be solving if I joined?"** — Shows you're impact-oriented
2. **"How does the team balance features vs tech debt?"** — Shows engineering maturity
3. **"What's something unique about working here?"** — Shows genuine interest, invites authentic answer

### Backup Questions
- "How do you ramp up new engineers?" — Good for understanding onboarding
- "What's the most frustrating part?" — Bold but shows you want honesty

## Category 1: Technical Work (16 questions)

| # | Question | Pick? |
|---|----------|-------|
| 1 | What are the engineering challenges that the company/team is facing? | ⭐ |
| 2 | What has been the worst technical blunder that has happened in the recent past? How did you deal with it? What changes were implemented afterwards? | ⭐ |
| 3 | What is the most costly technical decision made early on that the company is living with now? | ⭐ |
| 4 | What is the most fulfilling/exciting/technically complex project that you've worked on here so far? | ⭐ |

| 6 | How do you evaluate new technologies? Who makes the final decisions? | |
| 7 | How do you know what to work on each day? | |
| 8 | How would you describe your engineering culture? | |
| 9 | How has your role changed since joining the company? | |
| 10 | What is your stack? What is the rationale for/story behind this specific stack? | |
| 11 | Do you tend to roll your own solutions more often or rely on third party tools? | |
| 12 | How does the engineering team balance resources between feature requests and engineering maintenance? | |
| 13 | What do you measure? What are your most important product metrics? | |
| 14 | How often have you moved teams? What made you join the team you're on right now? | |
| 15 | What resources does the company have for new hires to study its product and processes? | |

## Anti-Patterns (Never Ask These)

- "What does the company do?" *(Shows you didn't research)*
- "How much vacation do I get?" *(Save for HR/offer stage)*
- "Can I work from home?" *(Ask about remote policy instead)*
- "Did I get the job?" *(Obviously)*
- Questions you could easily Google
