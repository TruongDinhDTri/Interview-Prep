## 5-Minute Pre-Interview Checklist

- [ ] Pick the version closest to this role
- [ ] Research company: 1 specific product/mission you genuinely admire
- [ ] Write a custom closing line with the company name + your specific reason
- [ ] Say it out loud once — check it's 45 seconds to 1 minute
- [ ] Prepare 1–2 follow-up answers for your spotlight project
- [ ] Smile before you start — warmth is contagious

---

## Delivery Essentials

- **Pace:** Slow on your name, numbers, and company name. Speed up on transitions.
- **Pauses:** After impact moments ("3,100 hours a year..." *pause*) and emotional beats.
- **Energy:** Warm and genuine for the opening, confident for the project, enthusiastic for the closing.
- **Personality:** Let your excitement show. You LOVE building things — let them hear it.
- **Filler words:** Replace "um/uh/like" with silence. Silence is confident.
- **Eye contact:** Look at camera (virtual) or interviewer (in-person) during closing.
- **If you blank:** Jump to your closing line — it's the last thing they remember.
