Fixed Window Counter

Timeline is divided into fixed windows. Each window has a counter. Limit: 3 requests per window. Click "Next Window" to move to a new time window.

Sliding Window Log

Keeps timestamps of all requests. Window: 60 seconds. Limit: 3 requests per window. Old timestamps are purged when they fall outside the window.
