## STAR Story Template

When creating STAR stories, use this structure:

```markdown
# [Story Title]

**Theme:** [Leadership / Conflict Resolution / Problem Solving / etc.]

## Situation
[Set the context - where, when, what was happening]

## Task
[What was your responsibility? What needed to be done?]

## Action
[What specific steps did YOU take? Focus on YOUR actions]

## Result
[What was the outcome? Quantify if possible. What did you learn?]

---

**Key Talking Points:**
- [Point 1]
- [Point 2]
- [Point 3]

**Potential Questions This Addresses:**
- [Question 1]
- [Question 2]
```

---

## Target: 5 Powerful STAR Stories

### Story Themes to Cover:

1. **Leadership / Taking Initiative**
   - Led a team or project
   - Took ownership of difficult situation

2. **Conflict Resolution / Teamwork**
   - Resolved disagreement with colleague
   - Worked through team challenges

3. **Problem Solving / Technical Challenge**
   - Solved complex technical problem
   - Overcame significant obstacle

4. **Failure / Learning from Mistakes**
   - Project that didn't go as planned
   - What you learned and how you grew

5. **Innovation / Going Above & Beyond**
   - Created something new
   - Exceeded expectations

---

## Common Behavioral Questions

### Must Prepare:
- Tell me about yourself
- Why do you want to work here?
- What's your greatest strength?
- What's your greatest weakness?
- Tell me about a time you faced a conflict with a coworker
- Describe a difficult project you worked on
- Give an example of when you showed leadership
- Tell me about a time you failed and what you learned
- How do you handle tight deadlines?
- Describe a disagreement with your manager
