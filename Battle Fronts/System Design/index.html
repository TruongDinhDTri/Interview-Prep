⚡ Key Numbers to Memorize

⏱ Latency Reference

L1 cache: 0.5ns · RAM: 100ns · SSD: 150µs · HDD: 10ms · Same DC: 500µs · Cross-continent: 150ms

📦 Storage Units

2^10=1KB · 2^20=1MB · 2^30=1GB · 2^40=1TB · 2^50=1PB · 1 char=1 byte · 1 photo≈1MB

📈 Availability SLA

99% → 3.65d/yr · 99.9% → 8.77h/yr · 99.99% → 52.6min/yr · 99.999% → 5.26min/yr

🔢 QPS Formula

Avg QPS = DAU × req/user ÷ 86,400 · Peak = Avg × 2–5× · 86,400 = seconds in a day
