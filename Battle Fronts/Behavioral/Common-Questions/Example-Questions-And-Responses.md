# 📖 Example Questions & Responses — TIH Rubric

*Source: [Tech Interview Handbook — Behavioral Interview Rubrics](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/behavioral-interview-rubrics/#example-questions-and-responses)*

> In a typical interview, they'll ask you **five or six questions** and dive deep into the details of each situation. Each question may provide signals on more than one focus area.

---

## How to Read the Scope Descriptions

The Junior / Senior / Staff descriptions are NOT full sample answers. They describe the **minimum scope of situation** the interviewer expects at each level. Your story must match or exceed your target level.

| Target Level | Scope Required |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Junior** | Impact on your team's focus area. Usually just you. |
| **Senior** | Impact on your entire team. Usually 3+ people. |
| **Staff** | Impact on your entire org. Usually 2+ teams. |

**Wiganz's target: Senior.** Every story needs 3+ people or team-wide impact.

---

## 1. Motivation

**Example Questions:**
- What project are you most proud of and why?
- Tell me about a recent day working that was really great and/or fun.

**Response Scope by Level:**
| Level | What They Expect |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Junior** | A story about a project they are proud of that had an impact on their team. |
| **Senior** | A story about a project they are proud of that had a **large** impact on their team. |
| **Staff** | A story about a project they are proud of that had a large impact on their **org**. |

**Wiganz's Story → Story 1: Jarvis-Bot or Story 3: Second Brain**
- Jarvis: 20+ people, 3,120 hrs/yr saved — large team-wide impact ✅ Senior
- Second Brain: 10 senior colleagues adopted it — team-wide impact ✅ Senior

---

## 2. Ability to be Proactive

**Example Questions:**
- Tell me about a time when you wanted to change something that was outside of your regular scope of work.
- Tell me about a time you had to make a fast decision and live with the results.

**Response Scope by Level:**
| Level | What They Expect |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Junior** | A story about a change they proactively suggested and drove that had an impact on their team's focus area. Usually only requiring the candidate themselves to work on. |
| **Senior** | A story about a change they proactively suggested and drove that had an impact on their **entire team**. Usually requiring **three or more people** to work on. |
| **Staff** | A story about a change they proactively suggested and drove that had an impact on their **entire org**. Usually requiring **two or more teams** to work on. |

**Wiganz's Story → Story 1: Jarvis-Bot or Story 2: JS-Injection**
- Jarvis: Self-initiated, adopted by 20+ across entire team ✅ Senior
- JS-Injection: Outside regular scope, adopted by full dev team ✅ Senior

---

## 3. Ability to Work in an Unstructured Environment

**Example Questions:**
- How do you decide what to work on next?
- Tell me about a project or task that was ambiguous or underspecified.

**Response Scope by Level:**
| Level | What They Expect |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Junior** | A story about an ambiguous task that the candidate took ownership of and was able to drive consensus from a few stakeholders in their team. Usually only requiring the candidate themselves to work on. |
| **Senior** | A story about an ambiguous project that the candidate took ownership of and was able to drive consensus from stakeholders in their **team or org**. Usually requiring **three or more people** to work on. |
| **Staff** | A story about an ambiguous project that the candidate took ownership of and was able to drive consensus from stakeholders in their **org**. Usually requiring **two or more teams** to work on. |

**Wiganz's Story → Story 2: JS-Injection or Story 5: Dependency Trap**
- JS-Injection: No spec, self-defined problem + solution, drove team adoption ✅ Senior
- Dependency Trap: Ambiguous crisis, no guidance, owned it solo then mentored 20+ ✅ Senior

---

## 4. Perseverance

**Example Questions:**
- Tell me about a time when you needed to overcome external obstacles to complete a task or project.
- Tell me about a time a project took longer than expected.

**Response Scope by Level:**
| Level | What They Expect |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Junior** | A story about a task with many technical difficulties and how they overcame each blocker. |
| **Senior** | A story about a project with many technical difficulties that were **blocking their team** and how they overcame each blocker. |
| **Staff** | A story about a project with many technical difficulties that were **blocking many teams** and how they overcame each blocker. |

**Wiganz's Story → Story 2: JS-Injection or Story 5: Dependency Trap**
- JS-Injection: CORS + CORB + CSP blockers, resolved for the whole team ✅ Senior
- Dependency Trap: Total crisis, rebuilt from scratch solo under deadline ✅ Junior–Senior

---

## 5. Conflict Resolution and Empathy

**Example Questions:**
- Tell me about a person or team who you found the most challenging to work with.
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a coworker.
- Tell me about a situation where two teams couldn't agree on a path forward.

**Response Scope by Level:**
| Level | What They Expect |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Junior** | A story about how they were able to work through a disagreement with a coworker on an **implementation detail** of a larger project. |
| **Senior** | A story about how they were able to work through a disagreement with a **few coworkers or team leads** on the **direction** of a larger project. |
| **Staff** | A story about how they were able to work through a disagreement with **two or more teams** on the direction of a large project. |

**Wiganz's Story → Story 4: The Conflict**
- Team Leader vs you, resolved with CEO present, professional outcome ✅ Senior

---

## 6. Growth

**Example Questions:**
- Describe a situation when you made a mistake, and what you learned from it.
- Tell me about some constructive feedback you received from a manager or a peer.
- Tell me about a skill set that you observed in a peer or mentor that you want to develop in the next six months.

**Response Scope by Level:**
| Level | What They Expect |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Junior** | A story about a new technology they want to learn and the progress they have made to learn it. |
| **Senior** | A story about a soft skill or technical skill they want to develop and the progress they have made. Usually a skill that will have the potential to **affect the entire team**. |
| **Staff** | A story about a soft skill or technical skill they want to develop and the progress they have made. Usually a skill that will have the potential to **affect two or more teams**. |

**Wiganz's Story → Story 5: Dependency Trap**
- Learned deep ownership from failure → mentored 20+ interns with new philosophy ✅ Senior

---

## 7. Communication

**No example questions** — assessed throughout the entire interview based on how clearly you explain your stories. Also overlaps with Empathy in how you communicate with others.

**What this means:** Every single answer you give is also a Communication assessment. Clear STARR structure, specificity, and natural delivery all contribute to this signal.

---

## After the Interview — Hire / No-Hire Decision

The hire/no-hire decision is given as a **spectrum** (low confidence → high confidence), not a binary.

| Level | Hire Threshold |
|-------|---------------|
| **Junior** | Positive signals on nearly all of the 8 focus areas. |
| **Senior** | Positive signals on nearly all of the 8 focus areas **AND** at the level expected for senior engineers. Otherwise → No Hire. |
| **Staff** | Positive signals on nearly all of the 8 focus areas **AND** at the level expected for staff engineers. Otherwise → No Hire at staff level (may still get a Senior hire recommendation). |

> **The implication:** A strong Junior-scope performance can still get you a hire — but at Junior level. If you're targeting Senior, your stories must consistently show team-wide (3+ people) impact.

---

## Quick Reference — Story Scope Checklist

Before telling any story, verify it hits Senior scope:

- [ ] Does it involve 3+ people? Or team-wide adoption?
- [ ] Did YOUR action drive a team-level outcome (not just your own work)?
- [ ] Is the impact large enough to matter to a team, not just a module?
