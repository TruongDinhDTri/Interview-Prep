# 30 Behavioral Questions Arsenal

**Source:** [Tech Interview Handbook — Most Common Behavioral Questions](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/behavioral-interview-questions/)

**Strategy:** Every question maps to 1-2 of your 5 STAR(R) stories. You never need to improvise — just pick the right story and emphasize the relevant angle.

## The 30 Questions (Exact TIH List)

### Category 1: Motivation & Self-Awareness (5 questions)

| # | Question | Primary Story | Angle to Emphasize |
|---|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Why do you want to work for X company? | — | Company research + map your values to theirs |
| 2 | Why do you want to leave your current/last company? | — | Growth-focused: "I've maximized my learning here and want bigger challenges." Don't badmouth. Mention desire for remote, better engineering culture, AI-native work. |
| 3 | What are you looking for in your next role? | **SB** | Growth, AI-native engineering, impact at scale, strong engineering culture |
| 4 | What are you excited about? | **SB** | AI augmenting developer productivity, building tools that multiply output |
| 5 | What frustrates you? | **JB** | Inefficiency and manual repetitive work — "I see automation opportunities everywhere" |

### Category 3: Conflict & Teamwork (5 questions)

| # | Question | Primary Story | Angle to Emphasize |

## Usage Tips

1. **Never memorize word-for-word** — know the story beats, adapt the emphasis
2. **Lead with the Result** if the question is about impact
3. **Lead with the Action** if the question is about process
4. **Always end with Reflection** — this is your seniority signal
5. **Time yourself:** S+T = 30 sec, A = 90 sec, R+R = 60 sec → Total ~3 min
6. **For non-story questions** (#12, #24, #25, #27, #28): prepare 2-3 bullet points, keep under 60 seconds
7. **"Why leave?"** (#2): NEVER badmouth. Focus on growth and what you want, not what's wrong.
