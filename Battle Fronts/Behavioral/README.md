## Framework: STAR(R)

| Stage | Weight | Time |
|-------|--------|------|
| **S**ituation | 10% | 15 sec |
| **T**ask | 10% | 10 sec |
| **A**ction | 50% | 90 sec |
| **R**esult | 15% | 15 sec |
| **R**eflection | 15% | 30 sec |

> The **Reflection** is what separates Senior from Junior responses.
