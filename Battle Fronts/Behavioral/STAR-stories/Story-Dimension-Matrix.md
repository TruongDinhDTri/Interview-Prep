# Story-Dimension Coverage Matrix

Based on [Tech Interview Handbook Rubrics](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/behavioral-interview-rubrics/), interviewers evaluate candidates across **8 dimensions**. Your 5 stories must collectively cover all 8.

---

## The 8 Evaluation Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What Interviewers Look For |
|---|-----------|---------------------------|
| 1 | **Motivation** | Self-driven passion, intrinsic desire to build and solve |
| 2 | **Proactivity** | Taking initiative without being asked, ownership |
| 3 | **Unstructured Environment** | Handling ambiguity, defining your own path |
| 4 | **Perseverance** | Pushing through obstacles, not giving up |
| 5 | **Conflict & Empathy** | Managing disagreements, perspective-taking |
| 6 | **Growth** | Self-awareness, learning from mistakes, improvement |
| 7 | **Communication** | Clear storytelling, explaining technical concepts |
| 8 | **Company Values** | Cultural alignment, shared principles |

## Seniority Scope Check

All stories should demonstrate **Senior-level scope** (multi-person/team impact):

| Story | Scope | Evidence |
|-------|-------|----------|
| S1: Jarvis-Bot | **Senior** | Team of 20+, company-wide adoption, management involvement |
| S2: JS-Injection | **Senior** | Became entire team's daily tool, affected 1,285+ vendor sites |

## Gap Mitigation Strategy

For dimensions with only one strong story:

1. **Motivation** — When telling Story 1 (Jarvis-Bot), add: "I spent weekends prototyping because I was genuinely excited about the impact"
2. **Growth** — When telling Story 3 (Second Brain), add: "This project pushed me to learn RAG architecture from scratch"
3. **Conflict** — Story 4 is strong enough to stand alone for all conflict questions
