## 6. COMPANY TAILORING GUIDE — 5-Minute Custom Closing

### Step 1: Research (2 minutes)
- Visit company website → find ONE product/feature you genuinely find interesting
- Check their engineering blog or "About" page for mission/values
- Look at the job description for what they emphasize

### Step 2: Connect (1 minute)
Ask yourself: "What does this company care about that I ALSO genuinely care about?"
- Build beautiful products? → Artist soul, Bubu Dudu
- Developer tools? → MagicTouch, SpeedUp, DX obsession
- AI/ML? → Multi-agent ecosystem, RAG search
- Scale? → 1,285+ vendors, deployment orchestration
- Startup speed? → 8 self-initiated tools, zero-to-one energy
- Data? → Scrapers, pipelines, batch processing

### Step 3: Write the Closing (2 minutes)
**Template:** "I'm excited about [Company] because [specific thing they do]. [Connect it to something you've done or believe]. I want to [what you'll bring]."

**Examples:**
- "I'm excited about Vercel because you're making deployment invisible — and after building deployment orchestration for 1,285 vendors, I know how hard and important that is."
- "I'm drawn to Linear because you treat developer tools as craft. I built 8 internal tools because I believe the same thing — great DX multiplies everything."
- "What excites me about Anthropic is that you're not just building AI — you're thinking about how to make it safe and useful. I built a multi-agent system because I believe AI should genuinely serve people, not just impress them."

### What NOT to Do
- ❌ "I'm looking for a team that values engineering craft" — too generic
- ❌ "I want to work somewhere with smart people" — everyone says this
- ❌ "I'm passionate about your mission" — without saying WHAT about the mission
- ✅ Name the company, name a specific product/feature, connect it to your own experience

## 7. TIH FRAMEWORK REMINDER — Structure & Rules

### The 3-Paragraph Structure
```
Paragraph 1: Name + background + what you love (PERSONALITY here!)
Paragraph 2: ONE impressive project with specifics (numbers, tech, who uses it)
Paragraph 3: Why THIS company — specific product/mission + connection to your values
```

### Rules
- **45 seconds to 1 minute** (~100-150 words)
- **3 clear paragraphs** — not a wall of text
- **ONE project per version** (maybe two if tightly related)
- **Lead with impact, not chronology** — don't start with "I graduated in..."
- **Sound HUMAN** — like you're talking to a friend, not reading a script
- **Numbers that stick** — one or two memorable metrics, not five
- **End with forward momentum** — you're excited about THEIR future, not just your past
- **Show personality** — use at least one identity hook per version

### What Makes TIH Examples Work
- They tell a mini-story ("I built X because Y, and now Z people use it")
- They show genuine enthusiasm ("I love...", "What excites me...")
- They're specific about the company ("I'm drawn to [Company] because [specific product]")
- They reveal something personal that makes the person memorable
